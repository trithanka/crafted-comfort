## Platform

web

## Users

- Homeowners, resort and hotel developers, café owners and architects across the eight Northeast Indian states who are weighing a large purchase: Loom Crafts outdoor furniture or a modular prefab cottage.
- Travellers and Guwahati locals looking for a stay (Barefoot in the Garden cottages, Crafted Comfort BnB suites), a meal, baked goods or tea.

## Product Purpose

Crafted Comfort, based in Bhetapara, Guwahati, Assam, is the authorized Northeast India partner of Loom Crafts. It sells premium outdoor furniture and modular prefab homes and cottages, and runs its own hospitality brands. Success is a qualified enquiry: a consultation, catalogue request, stay booking or table request.

## Positioning

Clients can see, stay in and use the products before they buy: the furniture and prefab cottages are in daily use at Crafted Comfort's own resort, Barefoot in the Garden in Sonapur. A catalogue-only dealer cannot offer this.

## Operating Context

- Brands: Loom Crafts (outdoor furniture and prefab, as partner), Barefoot in the Garden (Sonapur cottage resort; Guwahati dining branch upcoming; cloud kitchen and smart vending), Crafted Comfort BnB (Kahilapara, two suites, and a Sonapur villa), Nuts and Berries (bakery and café), Green Fog (tea lounge).
- Climate the products must survive: Northeast monsoon, high humidity, strong sun.
- Original site product lines: swings, sun loungers, sofa seating, dining, day beds and cabanas, accessories, prefab homes, fireplaces ("Calm by Fire"). Services: project planning, exterior and interior design, free annual maintenance, lifetime warranty.

## Product Principles

1. The home page opens with a short about Crafted Comfort and shows all its companies directly below (client requirement). The try-before-you-buy resort is the main argument after that.
2. Every page ends in one clear enquiry action.
3. Say specific, checkable things about materials, places and services; no marketing superlatives.
4. Each hospitality brand keeps its own character inside one shared system.

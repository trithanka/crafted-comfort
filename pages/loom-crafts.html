About Loom Crafts

Established in 2005 and headquartered in Ghaziabad, Loom Crafts is an Indian manufacturer of luxury hand-woven outdoor furniture and modular prefab homes. More than 800 craftsmen work in its 1-lakh sq ft facility, making durable, all-weather and often patented designs in wicker, rope and teak.

loomcrafts.com

Sofa sets, lounge chairs, poolside loungers, day beds, garden dining sets, swings, benches, cabanas, patio umbrellas and accessories for balconies, gardens, patios, terraces, pool areas and resorts. All-weather, rust-proof and low-maintenance, and widely used in hotels, resorts and luxury homes.

Verona modular sectional

Deep cushioned seating on an aluminium frame wrapped in braided rope. It rearranges into an L, a U or separate day beds.

Luxury prefab modular homes

Custom-designed modular homes and cottages in premium materials, made with precision to be stylish, sustainable and environmentally friendly. The standard design withstands heavy sun, winds up to 180 km/h, heavy rainfall and a 2 ft snow load, and weatherproof membranes protect every outside surface. With regular maintenance, the design life is 50+ years. Stay in one at Barefoot in the Garden before you order.

Made for monsoon country

What the pieces are made of, and why it matters in Assam's rain, humidity and sun.

  • Powder-coated aluminium

    Frames

    Aluminium does not rust, and the powder coat keeps its colour through wet seasons.

  • Teak and teak composite

    Tops and arms

    Dense timber and composite that shed water and resist termites.

  • Braided outdoor rope

    Weave

    Synthetic rope woven over the frame. It flexes, dries fast and does not sag.

  • Outdoor fabric

    Cushions

    Fade-resistant covers and quick-drying foam, so a cushion is dry again soon after the rain stops.
